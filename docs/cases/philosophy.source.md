# Public case snapshot: philosophy workflow

This is an anonymized, presentation-only summary of a read-only audit over a
private workflow built around a previously published philosophy paper. It does
not reproduce the paper, identify its author, disclose private reasoning, or
assert that any philosophical conclusion is true.

The audited artifact separated exact source anchors from interpretive
reconstruction and independent audit. Its frozen state contained 17 source
nodes, 40 reconstruction nodes, and 16 historical audit nodes. A later
reconstruction revision was accepted only after source-fidelity and
graph-structure review; superseded and rejected revisions remained inspectable.

A current read-only Chalxius audit found the stored workflow clean. The Fact
Graph contained zero admitted Facts and zero pending candidates. This is an
authority-boundary result, not a philosophical verdict. The underlying artifact
predates current unified-write activation and therefore remains read-only until
someone explicitly starts a future activation workflow.

The case also exercised two nontruth surfaces. A frozen Blackboard held
exploratory questions and challenges without mutating Paper or Audit authority.
A separate learning overlay mounted frozen research snapshots read-only; it
could store pedagogical structure but could not inherit truth or write back to
research data.
