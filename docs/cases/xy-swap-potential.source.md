# Public case snapshot: potential x-y interchange application

This is a presentation-only summary of a private predecessor archive studying
how exchanging the two coordinate functions of a spectral curve may affect
topological-recursion free energies. It does not disclose an unpublished
mathematical conclusion or reproduce private source material.

The predecessor workflow used a content-addressed dependency graph,
target-closure checks, packet-bound candidate submissions, independent reviews,
replayable computational evidence, and explicit revocation. At the audited
snapshot it contained 53 active legacy Facts, 95 dependency edges, a 21-target
closure, and one recorded revocation.

This is only a potential Chalxius application. The archive was not created with
Chalxius 0.3.5 and has not passed a current Chalxius workflow audit. Thirteen
legacy rounds fail newer source-evidence requirements, and inherited source
trust warnings remain. No current-format Paper store, Blackboard store, or
Chalxius Reader export was found in the predecessor project.

The relevant authors have not confirmed this case framing. Nothing here implies
their endorsement, validates a mathematical result, establishes publication
status, or recertifies a legacy Fact. Migration, source-evidence repair, fresh
review, and explicit author confirmation would be separate future work.
