# Chalxius v0.9.10 — Terminal Seal Hygiene

Chalxius 0.9.10 consolidates the frontier, Campaign, supervision, installation,
and host-filesystem repairs developed after 0.9.0. The release keeps Main's
operation surface compact while preserving exact graph identity, source and
mathematical boundaries, immutable history, and the independent Fact path.

It adds no automatic mathematical selector, dispatcher, persistent frontier
index, daemon, compatibility layer, migration ceremony, or new admission gate.

## Campaign and frontier continuity

Main can express a Campaign-target choice once when planning production or
repair. The same transaction updates one compact, replaceable nontruth working
state containing bounded active heads, recent attainment, mathematical
landmarks, and recovery roots. Canonical Research remains the owner of the
mathematical content.

The current frontier follows exact production, supervision, invalidation,
repair, and copy-on-write routes. Positive successors retire only the
intersecting predecessor route; unrelated parallel heads remain visible, and
overlapping supervision remains ingestible workflow rather than becoming a
second semantic head. Compatible concurrent rounds expose their exact in-flight
state instead of asking Main to repeat an already recorded choice.

Ordinary `search` and `show` navigate immutable Research as well as admitted
Facts. Stale Campaign heads, productive successors, clean review evidence, and
unmapped attention are distinguished rather than collapsed into one queue.
Main still owns semantic relevance and final dispatch.

## Source and supervision continuity

Structured source artifacts keep their typed primary-source role when projected
into a supervision card. Exact card-authorized path and SHA-256 bytes can be
used directly; locator prose and unbound files remain non-authorizing. This
removes duplicate byte-return ceremony without weakening source-use coverage or
review independence.

A copy-on-write successor is reviewed as a complete new product. Earlier
findings are attack seeds, not an allowlist, so PHX simplification never narrows
mathematical or source scrutiny.

## Terminal seal hygiene

macOS Finder may create `.DS_Store` while a terminal bundle is browsed. Earlier
validation treated that writable host decoration as sealed authority and could
invalidate a valid product even when every expected SHA-256 still matched.

Terminal validation now ignores only a regular `.DS_Store` at the bundle root
or its exact `artifacts` directory. Every seal, return, and artifact remains
path-, hash-, containment-, and permission-bound. Unknown files, unexpected
directories, writable authority bytes, symlinks, hard links, special entries,
device crossings, and byte drift still fail.

## Frontier performance

A bounded frontier read previously rebuilt the complete Research COW/repair
adjacency once per workgroup and revalidated the same assignment product and
ingestion receipt multiple times inside one command.

The command now shares one ephemeral exact-snapshot inspection context across
its internal projections. Research or round publication invalidates affected
state before a later read. Nothing is persisted, and output, selection,
dispatch, Candidate, and Fact semantics are unchanged.
