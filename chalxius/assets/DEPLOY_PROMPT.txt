Chalxius 1.0.2 — Capability Budget Audit Repair

This is a deployment and runtime-handoff guide. It is not mathematical
evidence and has no Research, Candidate, Certification, Gateway, or Fact
authority.

The 1.0.2 validator gives each structured Repair capability manifest its own
existing 64 MiB budget while retaining one command-local read-once byte and
path/digest-conflict cache. Historical validity is therefore independent of
full-ledger traversal order. One oversized manifest, a conflicting declaration,
a no-follow violation, or hash-drifted bytes still fails closed. Installation
does not rewrite frozen project data.

Prospective Fact admission now certifies exact whole Research records instead
of constructing a second mathematical graph. Main records important Research
ids with `fact-frontier-mark`; `fact-frontier` derives current COW terminals,
certification state, certified heads, and batch opportunities live. At a
natural research window Main explicitly freezes selected marks with
`plan-fact-packaging`. No timer, threshold, scheduler, automatic dispatcher, or
truth inference is installed.

For prospective clean Research, a supervisor may already emit the exact
semi-formal interface or an explicit `needs_split` disposition. An all-ready
plan seals mechanically; historical, missing, or custom interface work retains
the narrow packaging-agent fallback. The interface conclusion must equal the
existing top-level Research claim; assumptions, domain/types, quantifiers,
explicit certified predecessor Research ids, and limitations make the claim
callable without rewriting the proof. One independent verifier then
checks every Research record, dependency edge, and interface. A mechanical
Gateway may certify exactly the components marked correct. A failing sibling
component does not discard an independent correct component.

A prospective schema-v3 split repair uses one worker-chosen finite batch rather
than one assignment per successor. Ingestion stages all members and one owner,
then commits the batch last; uncommitted staged records are not frontier or Fact
work. One supervisor per scope reviews the complete committed membership.

Fact is therefore an append-only certification property of Research. Explicit
certified predecessor ids and exact grant hashes form its rigid dependency
subtree; descriptive `relation` prose has no proof effect. A later COW leaves
history intact and makes the replaced grant and certified descendants
`needs_reverification`. A minor verifier finding returns a complete COW to the
same verifier without an ordinary supervisor; a fundamental error returns to
ordinary Research. The verifier may not author either repair. Old Candidate,
adverse, Certification, and Fact bytes retain their original read-only
authority and are not automatically migrated.

Campaign working memory now carries up to sixteen Main-selected heads per
Target through legacy checkpoint reading, current state, plan-round, explicit
reconciliation, and live projection. The routine Target window is separate.
Head claims retain up to 300 characters, root claims 400, and projected
historical claims 320; complete Research remains retrievable by exact id. Do
not turn this bounded breadth into paging, automatic head selection, or a
scheduler.

Routine Campaign frontier now preserves every active head and supervision scope
in one compact mathematical/action index: concise claim, current-route Research
ids, next action, and exact actionable Research/round ids. Repeated forensic
route summaries, terminal/evidence sets, hashes, empty fields, and synonymous
state remain available with `--diagnostic`.

Use the Main semantic **摸石头过河** during live research: continue research by
default; when a material architecture failure threatens operation, save the
exact Campaign/Research/round/return boundary, perform the bounded integrated
repair and local installation, reconcile those bytes, and resume the same
mathematical boundary. Do not turn this into a monitor, timer, scheduler,
automatic issue trigger, automatic installer, publication action, or truth
gate.

Default supervision planning now subtracts exact completed or in-flight scopes
and returns a nonmutating no-op when every applicable scope is already covered.
Routine `frontier --limit N` bounds the nested Campaign goal projection while
retaining complete counts, digests, ids, and concise mathematics; use
`--diagnostic` for the full topology. Task-card source assurance and return
validation share one structural direct-source predicate for standalone
`primary` and exact `authoritative_source` roles. Explicit scope conflicts,
malformed source roles, and all truth boundaries remain strict.

Semantic COW terminals determine whether an older workgroup is complete or
ambiguous; they do not replace that workgroup's assignment, round, product,
component, or supervision identity. Frontier projects operational actions from
the exact physical members and follows a product back to its source assignment
only through validated frozen provenance. This keeps COW history intact while
preventing one repair route from hiding another route's real work state.

Candidate enforcement and Main's frontier share one exact supervision coverage
projection. Main sees completed, missing, pending, conflicting, and unsafe
scopes directly; only malformed or inaccessible bytes are lineage-unreadable.
Candidate remains strict, and no scheduler, automatic dispatch, compatibility
layer, Candidate effect, or Fact effect is added.

Before editing or packaging a proposed source root, run
`scripts/candidate_identity.py --root ROOT --installed-root INSTALLED`. Select
from its exact VERSION, manifest, worktree, and installed-difference evidence;
directory names are diagnostic only. The projection never switches branches,
mutates a tree, or installs bytes.

Default `mgraph frontier` is Main's compact decision surface. A Campaign
`research_goal` keeps its exact same-Campaign Research root as immutable
provenance, while Main's newest nontruth checkpoint supplies current work heads
and attained boundaries. Completion of those heads does not close the semantic
goal: it requests Main disposition or a new exact head. Only explicit archive
removes the goal. No note dispatches, schedules, closes, or admits anything,
and the unscoped workflow queue remains global.

Typed products, supervision plans/results, and structured repairs are operated
through their existing provenance, receipt, kind, and exact lineage fields;
relation strings remain concise Main context rather than a second capability
vocabulary. A structured repair may use an exact Main synthesis trigger whose
complete research-source set equals its related ids, so repaired review inputs
do not remain parallel terminal challenges. Malformed, cross-Campaign,
backward-time, ambiguous, incomplete-product, and unsupervised repair routes do
not become completed work.

`round-status` exposes the attacked source assignment and product ids on each
supervision assignment. Main compares those ids with actual live authorship
before reusing a reviewer. An independent reviewer may be reused across scopes
or COW stages; an attacked product's author may not self-review it. This is a
read-only dispatch aid, not an identity registry or gate.

Exact legacy Main-authored COW roots remain directly operable when their
existing relation, complete source-id set, one completed production product,
challenge companions, Campaign, dependencies, and chronology determine one
unambiguous repair edge. In a multi-head goal, real `await_return` work is the
foreground goal state even when an older sibling still needs reconciliation;
all sibling actions remain visible. One command reuses already hash-validated
Research envelope bytes before full record/artifact validation rather than
rereading the same immutable file.

Routine goal rows preserve exact actions and bounded current, recent, and
historical mathematical summaries. Repeated forensic topology remains behind
explicit diagnostic output. A target-bound production plan may hand off an
old active route through its exact completed terminal descendants; unrelated
parallel heads remain visible and Main still makes the mathematical choice.
Batch round status ignores only recognized private staging names and reports
their count, while visible malformed entries remain diagnostic.

When a downstream production explicitly cites an upstream product or review,
its source-scope supervisor may follow that exact workflow provenance to the
completed upstream source review. The card carries the review and only the
primary-source hashes it actually cited. It never infers semantic relevance,
reopens the review, or scans the project for a substitute.

One frontier invocation shares one ephemeral exact-snapshot inspection of
Research COW/repair topology, completion, and assignment product/receipt
bindings across its internal projections. Research or round publication
invalidates the affected state. No persistent cache, index, daemon, scheduler,
or workflow authority is installed.

Main can bind a new Research root to one explicit Campaign atomically with
`memory-add --campaign`. New production manifests preserve the exact selection
source, Research ids, Campaign, mode, and replay argv. Frontier rows expose
next attention and its exact Research/round basis, and they show live
supervision before any source-product safety recommendation. The former Brave
Future and goal-intake commands have no alias; historical sidecar bytes remain
inert nontruth provenance.

CHX liveness is explicit: Main supplies current run ids when it needs a live
projection. Old open ledgers appear as bounded orphaned history until a
content-addressed administrative disposition marks them abandoned,
superseded, or administratively complete. That marker never resolves or hides
an issue and never changes the global issue snapshot.

Campaign history remains append-only and has no routine length ceiling.
`campaign-status` returns one compact current state, one latest minimal
frontier checkpoint, and a fixed recent event-summary tail; it has no paging
or cursor mode. Exact older bytes remain in the Campaign ledger for targeted
forensics. New checkpoints contain routing ids and concise disposition only.
Campaign-scoped rounds freeze active semantics plus an exact history-prefix
commitment, so later append is valid but prefix mutation is not. Repairs
inherit the source Research Campaign in both the new Research and its round;
an unbound source never infers `ACTIVE`.

Attained Campaign checkpoints now expose only exact, uncheckpointed terminal
production/supervision/COW successor routes to Main, with bounded ids plus full
counts and digests. This view never updates a checkpoint or selects work.
Checkpoint active heads are compared with the same exact successor graph.
Stale heads, productive current routes, clean terminal review evidence, and a
nonblocking refresh recommendation are shown separately; Main alone decides
whether and how to write the next checkpoint. Ordinary Main `search` and
`show` navigate exact immutable Research as well as verified Facts.
An ingested product projects lifecycle action through its exact production
root, and in-flight supervision remains attached to that stable root. Deep
legacy repair/COW relations are traversed only from exact recorded Research
relations. When active Campaign goals exist, goal-unmapped historical work is
shown in a bounded secondary attention summary rather than filling the primary
workflow queue; it remains fully available for Main's exact search and
promotion.
During new round construction, an exact existing 12-hex Research id literally
named in the selected Research prose is frozen with its record and artifact
capabilities; unknown checksum-like tokens remain inert. No fuzzy semantic
inference, compatibility layer, dispatcher, or new authority is introduced.

Artifact silence, one quiet wait, elapsed time, context compaction, bounded
startup reading, and deep reasoning do not prove worker loss. Main checks fresh
ordinary host status, messages, tool errors, and round bytes. Repeated no-useful-
output milestones may justify reclaiming a live-but-unproductive worker;
reassignment requires explicit failure or sustained total nonresponse
corroborated beyond artifact silence. This creates no timer, watcher, heartbeat,
receipt, or recovery state.

A copy-on-write successor is a new complete product. Prior findings and repair
specifications are mandatory but non-exhaustive attack seeds, not a defect
allowlist. Each supervisor freshly reviews the complete successor surface and
conserved claims inside its exact assigned proof, source, program-math, or
integration scope. PHX never narrows that scrutiny.

Production mode selects only the sparse automatic supervision default. Main
may explicitly choose any registered primary scope that fits the complete
frozen successor; the choice is optional and never becomes a duplicate-review
or admission gate.

For a frontier stated as a specific named mathematical object, class, theorem,
or exact identifier, Main performs one bounded exact Research search over its
stable names and identifiers before card freeze, reviews completed production,
repair successors, and applicable supervision, and then chooses relevant
`related_research_ids`. This is an instruction, not an automatic expansion,
index, receipt, state, scheduler, or gate.

Main owns cross-round and copy-on-write search and final dispatch. Scouts only
collect bounded evidence. After mathematical dispatch or receipt, inspect
visible free slots and keep at least two workers active only when independent,
nonduplicative, high-value mathematical boundaries really exist. For ordinary
engineering, delegate only when a bounded independent task remains faster
after startup, context-transfer, coordination, and merge costs; never create
filler or a slot quota.
A client "Reconnecting..." banner is transport state, not worker or round state.
After reconnect, inspect agents, canonical return bytes, ingestion, and round
state; never duplicate or reclaim solely from the banner, and add no reconnect
gate or liveness scheduler.
If a host command has yielded a still-running process/session without a final
exit code or expected JSON, resume that same session. Do not call the first
yield blank stdout and do not retry a mutating planner for output. Only a
completed command with missing or invalid required output is an output defect.

Ordinary Research operates exact admitted Fact premises directly and does not
replay unrelated admission history. A frozen task-card related artifact whose
role declares the standalone token `primary` may be used directly by
`source_uses` without a copied return artifact. Existing returned-source
bindings remain valid; toy checks and bridges remain return-bound.

## Normal global local installation

For an explicitly authorized ordinary host-global update, run:

```bash
PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 python3 -B \
  /absolute/path/to/candidate/scripts/local_install.py
```

The installer validates the complete candidate manifest, runs the candidate
self-test and the focused route/install regressions, archives the prior
runtime, atomically replaces `~/.codex/skills/chalxius`, self-tests the
installed copy, and retains one immediate rollback tree at
`~/.codex/skill-rollbacks/chalxius-current`.

It performs zero project reads and zero project writes. Do not require a
project audit, protected-project inventory, worker receipt, release matrix,
full test suite, or mutation audit merely to install a routine local candidate.
Use `--dry-run` when the same candidate validation is needed without changing
the installed runtime.

If the post-swap self-test fails, the installer restores the prior runtime.
Historical archives are validation data only: never import or execute them,
never use them as a worker runtime, and never rewrite frozen cards or MathGraph
nodes.

## Protected-project or forensic cutover

Use `scripts/runtime_cutover.py` only when the user explicitly requests a
protected-project or forensic deployment. That command retains its project
inventory, exact validation receipt, and deeper evidence requirements. It is
not the default global installation route.

Publication, remote push, tagging, and merge require their own explicit user
authorization. Installation alone does not authorize any of them.

## Runtime and route boundaries

Ordinary graph work is governed by MathGraph hashes, dependencies, provenance,
workflow stage, and owner boundaries. Runtime paths, archive locations, and
obsolete receipt metadata are diagnostic provenance; they do not block valid
graph reads or append-only nontruth work.

`route_invalidations` names exact stale Research targets. The broad
`related_research_ids` field may preserve source, contextual, or provenance
links, but it is not an invalidation-transmission relation. A later semantic
closure rule must be modeled and tested as a distinct explicit relation.

## Mathematical workflow boundary

The prospective truth path is:

`Research -> frozen package -> independent verifier decision -> Gateway Research certification`

Production is constructive. Supervision is the later logical review subround;
it may begin as soon as its own component is ready and need not wait for
unrelated production. Prospective Fact packaging, one independent verifier,
and Gateway certification retain separate owners. Administrative receipts are optional
workflow markers, not a second truth path.

For a current computation, bind formula-to-code, mathematical domain,
representation, approximation/truncation convention, output interpretation,
and an independent mathematical check. Do not allocate a computation merely to
repeat an already available derivation.

CHX records architecture-caused or architecture-amplified failures. PHX is
advisory global route memory. Neither is Fact authority or a reason to block
otherwise valid mathematical work. Prefer removing duplicate work and
accidental coupling over adding another gate, cache, or lifecycle mechanism.
