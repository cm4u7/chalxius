Release: `0.4.0-chalk`.

This file records the additive Paper Logic/Audit Graph boundary. The global
workflow-evidence V4 policy revision remains `mathgraph-0.3.0`; existing V4
projects and evidence are not rewritten.

## Release claims

1. A managed `paper_logic/` store is optional. Its absence is a clean,
   backward-compatible state.
2. Exact source, researcher reconstruction, paper audit, agent exploration,
   and admitted facts are separate planes.
3. Literal claims, operator ledgers, exact target/evidence anchors, inference
   ports, coverage, and clean-context reviews are fail-closed.
4. Philosophy audits distinguish exact logical effect from broader dialectical
   effect and distinguish repair from refutation. Mathematics retains the
   exact-counterexample standard.
5. Wrong audit nodes are challenged and superseded through new immutable
   revisions; old snapshots remain historical.
6. Agents have deterministic full-object graph queries and an optional
   governed full-fidelity Blackboard mirror. Reserved mirror objects cannot be
   created by generic/worker writes or promoted directly.
7. All new objects have `truth_effect="none"` and cannot be Fact Graph
   predecessors.
8. Danus and Chalk remain mutually exclusive research runtimes, while Grill Me
   may co-mount exact Danus facts and immutable Chalk `pls-*` / `bbs-*`
   snapshots in a separate nontruth learning overlay. Pedagogy never enters a
   Chalk review or source store.
