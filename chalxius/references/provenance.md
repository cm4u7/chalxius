# Provenance and licensing

Local MathGraph is an independent lightweight implementation informed by the public Danus design.
Its fact Markdown and 16-hex logical content-id contract are Danus-compatible; its schema-v2/v3
submission, packet, immutable review, acceptance, round, and receipt formats and its external-source
evidence-v2/v3 critical audits and v3 tiered source-audit reuse are local hardening extensions and
should not be attributed to upstream Danus.

Primary reference: Jihao Liu et al., *Danus: Orchestrating Mathematical Reasoning Agents with
Fact-Graph Memory*, arXiv:2607.06447, version 2, 8 July 2026. Upstream repository:
`frenzymath/Danus`.

This bundle contains no upstream model-service code, credentials, or problem graph. See the bundled
`LICENSE` (Apache License 2.0) and `NOTICE.md` for distribution and attribution information.
